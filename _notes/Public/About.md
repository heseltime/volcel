What I care about most these days doesn't do well on Instagram or LinkedIn. It is quieter and more local: it's church music and crib building, it's choir and theatre, it's the village where I grew up. Life slow and meditative, reflective.

I'm a software engineer at Dynatrace, working in observability: making large, busy systems legible to the people who run them, by way of a Semantic Dictionary that operationalizes conventions by way of modelling and pipelining. My background is literature and film in New York as well as software engineering and AI in Linz.[^road]

## Why "volcel"

Short for *voluntarily celibate*, meant lightly. It points at a journey toward a religious community and a life that takes prayer and work equally seriously, informed by the university prototype of such a community.[^khg] I don't know yet where it ends; this is one place where I walk it out loud: the Jesuit[^jesuits] novitiate is currently likely and will be documented here if/when we get there.

My village, Altmünster, is named after the old abbey recorded there (somewhere in Upper Austria, central Europe) already in 909, and its church is dedicated to St. Benedict.[^minster] Along the shore of a cold, deep lake at the foot of the alps is Traunkirchen, where the Jesuits held the old convent from 1622 until 1773. A current teaching engagement for high schoolers brings me there more often again, just go to my home page for more details/if interested.

## A bus ride

In C.S. Lewis's *The Great Divorce*, a bus lifts off from a gray town where it always rains and carries its passengers to foothills so real the grass hurts their feet.[^lewis] What stays with me is the ride itself - having left one place and not yet arrived at the other, on a bus like any old bus in the world, where these ideas might spark, but suddenly: other-worldly. 

So this is what these pages are for: a place between work, creative time and spiritual life, where writing has a real chance to declutter how I think about some topics, more room for creativity and inner space - in a kind of lattice structure along a horizontal "reading-model" to [[find a format::https://github.com/heseltime/volcel]] that can fit the content well.

My current day job and this inner work ask similar questions. What is signal and what is noise? Where does attention actually go? And could the habits of observability (instrument, look, simplify, look again) help declutter a life, or would that just add another dashboard? That thread starts at [[Observability for Inner Space]].
